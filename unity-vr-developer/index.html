    FAQ

    Questions studios ask

    Which headsets do you develop for?

    Mainly Meta Quest, the most common standalone headset for training and consumer VR. Pico is supported too, and OpenXR lets one Unity project target several headsets when needed.

    Can VR training include multiplayer?

    Yes. I have built multiplayer VR with Photon and PlayFab, and the same approach lets an instructor and several trainees share one session.

    Do you also create the 3D art?

    I focus on development. I work with your artists or the assets you provide, and I can suggest asset sources to get a prototype moving quickly.

    How long does a VR prototype take?

    A focused prototype usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on scope and how much content already exists.

    How do you price this kind of work?

    Fixed scope work is quoted per milestone after a short scoping call. Ongoing work is billed monthly. Share your budget range in the form and I will tell you honestly what it can cover.
