    Questions studios ask

    Can you help with one task or a complete game?

    Yes. I can join an existing project for a defined task, build a mobile or web game from concept to launch, or develop an interactive VR experience. Share your scope and timeline for a tailored quote.

    Can you join our team on a contract?

    Yes. I regularly work as an embedded senior Unity developer on a monthly or milestone contract, joining your repository, task board and stand ups. Tell me about your team and timeline and we can agree on scope.

    Do I need to choose Unity before contacting you?

    No. Tell me what you want people to play or interact with and where it needs to run. Unity is my core specialty, and we can settle the technical approach once the requirements are clear.

    How long does game development take?

    A simple prototype usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. A full store ready mobile game typically takes 6 to 12 weeks, depending on features like multiplayer or backends.

    Do I own the source code?

    Yes. You receive the complete Unity source code with documentation, and full ownership transfers to you on delivery.

    Do you build multiplayer games and backends?

    Yes. I use Photon Fusion, PUN and FishNet for real time multiplayer, and PlayFab and Firebase for live backends, leaderboards and in game economies.

    Can you publish to Google Play and the App Store?

    Yes. I handle store submission to Google Play and the App Store, or ship WebGL builds for the web, including ads and analytics setup.
