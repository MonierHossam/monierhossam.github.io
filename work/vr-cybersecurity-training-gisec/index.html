    VR cybersecurity training game for Meta Quest, shown at GISEC Global Dubai

    An educational VR game for Meta Quest 2, built in Unity with Photon multiplayer and a PlayFab backend. It was showcased at GISEC Global, the cybersecurity event in Dubai, in April 2024.

    The challenge

    The goal was to teach cybersecurity through play, in VR, in time to show it to visitors at a major industry event. Event demos leave no room for setup problems. The experience has to run smoothly on a standalone headset, make sense to people trying VR for the first time, and support the multiplayer features the client needed.

    What I built

    • Educational VR gameplay for Meta Quest 2 in Unity
    • Multiplayer sessions networked with Photon
    • PlayFab backend integration
    • A build ready for live demos on the GISEC Global show floor

    Results

    • Showcased at GISEC Global in Dubai, April 2024
    • Built for the same client as the WebGL phishing awareness game, which has its own case study
