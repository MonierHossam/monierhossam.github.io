    FAQ

    Questions studios ask

    Do you work with Photon Fusion 2?

    Yes. Photon Fusion 2 is part of my core stack, alongside PUN 2 and FishNet for projects that need a different networking model.

    Can you take over a multiplayer game another developer started?

    Yes. I start with a short audit of the existing code, then fix the riskiest problems first so the game stays playable while we improve it.

    Should we use shared mode or host mode?

    It depends on your game. Shared mode is simpler and suits casual and social games. Host or server mode gives you authority over the game state, which competitive games usually need. I recommend one after looking at your mechanics, player counts and budget.

    How do you price this kind of work?

    Fixed scope work is quoted per milestone after a short scoping call. Ongoing work is billed monthly. Share your budget range in the form and I will tell you honestly what it can cover.

    Where are you based and when are you available?
