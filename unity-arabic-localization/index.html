    FAQ

    Questions studios ask

    Why does Arabic text look broken in Unity?

    Many Unity text setups do not shape Arabic letters or handle right to left order by default, so words appear disconnected and reversed. Fixing it needs a shaping step and RTL aware layout, which is exactly what I implement.

    Do you work with TextMeshPro, UI Toolkit or both?

    Both. My Football Manager 2026 work was in Unity UI Toolkit, and many mobile games use TextMeshPro with an RTL extension. I pick the approach that fits your project.

    Can you also translate the text?

    I am a native Arabic speaker and can review translations for accuracy and tone. For large volumes of text I work alongside your translation vendor and make sure their work displays correctly in game.

    Will adding Arabic break my English UI?

    It should not. I build RTL support so each language gets the correct direction and layout, and I test both before handing over.

    How do you price this kind of work?

    Fixed scope work is quoted per milestone after a short scoping call. Ongoing work is billed monthly. Share your budget range in the form and I will tell you honestly what it can cover.
